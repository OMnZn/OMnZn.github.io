### Project Overview
Addressing the issue of existing models relying on black-box feature mappings, I explored and designed the **L20R** and **ELEVar** network architectures in collaboration with the team. 

### My Contributions
* **Architectural Design:** Attempted to introduce prior knowledge from statistics and structural biology to explicitly model protein epistasis from a mathematical perspective.
* **Performance & Interpretability:** Enhanced the interpretability of the model and achieved solid predictive performance on several public benchmarks.
