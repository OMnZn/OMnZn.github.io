### Project Overview
Reviewed and compared the formats and label definitions of multi-source public mutation datasets, and participated in the development of the automated data cleaning tool **MutCleaner** to address data heterogeneity.

### My Contributions
* **Data Standardization:** Achieved validity verification and format standardization for multi-source data, constructing unified training and testing sets for deep learning models.
* **Open Source Contribution:** This tool has been published on PyPI and is currently under review at *Bioinformatics*.

*(Note: [View on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/mutcleaner/) | [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/xulab-research/MutCleaner))*
