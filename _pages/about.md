Hi, I am Mengxin Yang, an undergraduate student majoring in Statistics at [Zhongnan University of Economics and Law (ZUEL)](https://www.zuel.edu.cn), expected to graduate in June 2027. 

Currently, I work as a Research Assistant at [Xu Lab](https://github.com/xulab-research). My research primarily lies in AI for Science (AI4S) and Computational Biology. I aim to integrate biochemical prior knowledge and mathematical reasoning into deep learning models. 

My recent work focuses on protein mutation effect prediction (specifically $$\Delta\Delta G$$ and $$\Delta T_m$$), AI-assisted protein directed evolution, and the application of Graph Neural Networks in biology. I am particularly interested in multi-constraint protein generation, leveraging large-scale protein data and biological priors to optimize protein design. Additionally, I have practical experience in data engineering, including the cleaning and standardization of both economic and biomolecular datasets.

I am seeking to pursue a Ph.D. degree in Computational Biology in Hong Kong, aiming to further explore the intersection of mathematics, machine learning, and biology. In modeling, I value not only the generalization capability but also the interpretability—understanding *why* a model is effective.

I am always open to discussions. If you share similar research interests, please feel free to reach out via email.
